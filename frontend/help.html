Getting Started

1

Create Your Profile

Set up your account with your school information and teaching subjects. This helps personalize your experience and connects you with relevant content.

2

Add Your Courses

Start by adding the courses or classes you teach. Navigate to "My Courses" and click "Add New Course" to get started. You can organize your questions and quizzes by course.

3

Create Questions

Build your question bank by creating multiple choice, true/false, or short answer questions. Use the AI Question Generator to speed up the process, or manually create questions through the Question Creator.

4

Build Quizzes

Create quizzes by selecting questions from your question bank. You can reorder questions with drag-and-drop, assign point values, and customize answer spacing for printed versions.

5

Share and Collaborate

Share your quizzes with other teachers in the community or export them for use in your classroom. Browse the community library to find and copy quizzes created by other educators.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new quiz?

To create a new quiz:

  • Click "Create New Quiz" from your Dashboard or Quiz Manager
  • Enter a quiz name and select the course it belongs to
  • Click "Create Quiz" to start adding questions
  • Use the "Add Questions" button to select from your question bank
  • Drag and drop to reorder questions as needed

How does the AI Question Generator work?

The AI Question Generator uses advanced AI to automatically create questions based on your content. You can upload documents or paste text, select the question types you want, and the AI will generate relevant questions for you to review and edit before adding to your question bank.

Can I edit questions after adding them to a quiz?

Yes! Click the edit icon next to any question in your quiz to open the Question Creator and make changes. The changes will be saved to the question in your question bank and will update in all quizzes that use that question.

How do I print a quiz for my students?

From the Quiz Manager or when editing a quiz, click either "Print Student Version" or "Print Answer Key". You'll see a preview where you can make final adjustments before printing. The student version includes blank space for answers, while the answer key shows the correct answers.

Can I share quizzes with other teachers?

Absolutely! You can share your quizzes by clicking the "Share" button in Quiz Manager. This generates a link you can send to other teachers. They can browse your shared quiz in the Community section and copy it to their own account to use or modify.

How do I organize my questions by topic?

When creating a question, you'll assign it to a specific Subject, Topic, Subtopic, and Section. This hierarchical organization helps you filter and find questions later. For example: Subject: English → Topic: Romeo & Juliet → Subtopic: Act 3 → Section: Scene 4.

What question types are supported?

EduShare currently supports three question types:

  • Multiple Choice: Questions with 2-6 answer options
  • True/False: Simple binary questions
  • Short Answer: Open-ended questions where students write their own response

How do I control the answer space on printed quizzes?

For short answer questions, you can adjust the number of lines printed for student responses. When editing a quiz, use the lines selector next to each short answer question to choose between 3, 5, 10, 15, or 20 lines of writing space.
